Problem Setup

Complex Multi-Use Building Optimization: This demo optimizes space allocation for 7 different land uses (Restaurant, Residential, Retail, Office, Cafe, Gym, Green Space) across 6 time periods throughout the day. The solver balances 3 competing objectives:
• Minimizing overcrowding (quadratic penalty)
• Minimizing underutilization (linear penalty)
• Minimizing annual operating costs

With 7 decision variables, 42 demand-capacity checks (7 uses × 6 periods), and non-linear constraints, this creates a challenging multi-objective problem space.
